Lift hard. Walk a lot. Hit your protein.
Manage hunger, don't fight it. Stay consistent. Repeat.

Weekly Cardio

  • 2–3 incline treadmill sessions (15–20 min, after lifting)
  • 1 Zone 2 session (25–35 min) — treadmill, StairMaster, bike, or rower
  • 8,000–12,000 steps most days
  • Occasional HIIT is fine if you enjoy it — it's not the foundation of the fat-loss plan

Progression

When you hit the top of the rep range with good form on every set:

  • Increase weight by the smallest available increment next workout
  • Can't increase weight yet? Add one rep per set until you can

Weekly Check-In — Every Sunday

  • Progress photos — front, side, back

  • Hunger (1–10)

Weight/measurements stalled 2–3 weeks?

Reduce calories slightly (~100–150 kcal/day) or add a bit more walking.

Strength dropping, or constantly exhausted?

Increase calories slightly or reduce cardio.

Key Reminders

  • The "lean" look comes from losing enough body fat while keeping muscle — not from eating as little as possible
  • Feeling hungrier when training harder is normal. Manage it with filling foods (protein, vegetables, fruit, potatoes, oats) rather than fighting it
  • Consistency over 8 weeks beats constantly changing your routine
