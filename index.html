  • Progress every week

HIIT & Quad Growth

Will sprinting make my quads big?

No. Big quads mainly come from heavy squats, heavy leg press, heavy quad-focused training, and a calorie surplus. HIIT mainly builds conditioning and burns calories.

To keep legs lean: limit HIIT to 2 sessions/week, prioritize incline walking, and keep lower-body strength work glute/hamstring focused.

Reminders

  • Muscle memory helps you regain strength and definition quickly
  • Fat loss comes mainly from nutrition — training preserves muscle
  • Consistency beats perfection
  • Goal isn't to be exhausted every workout — it's to improve every week
  • Trust the process for the full 3 weeks before changing anything

Once back at Queen's with a full gym, switch to a more advanced 5-day plan using cables and machines to maximize a lean, athletic look while minimizing unnecessary quad growth.
